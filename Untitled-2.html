West Bengal Travel Route Map

Popular tourist circuits with suggested step-by-step travel paths

Route 1: North Bengal Hill Circuit

Kolkata ➔ NJP / Bagdogra ➔ Kurseong ➔ Darjeeling ➔ Kalimpong

Highlights: Tiger Hill sunrise, Batasia Loop, Toy Train, tea garden walks.

Route 2: Dooars Wildlife Circuit

Siliguri ➔ Lataguri ➔ Gorumara ➔ Jaldapara ➔ Buxa

Highlights: One-horned Rhinos, Elephant safari, dense sal forests, river watchtowers.

Route 3: Heritage & Terracotta Circuit

Kolkata ➔ Shantiniketan ➔ Bishnupur ➔ Murshidabad

Highlights: Tagores Ashram, Baluchari sarees, Hazarduari Palace, Terracotta temples.

Route 4: Coastal Beach Circuit

Kolkata / Howrah ➔ Digha ➔ Shankarpur ➔ Mandarmani ➔ Tajpur

Highlights: Water sports, beach sunset, red crab beaches, peaceful seaside resorts.
